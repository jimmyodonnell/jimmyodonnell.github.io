| ---------- | ------ |
| 1 | garlic clove |
| 1 tsp | anchovy paste |
| 1 tsp | dijon mustard |
| 1 tsp | Worcestershire sauce |
| 15-30 g | lemon juice |
| 25-50 g | parmesan cheese |
| 120 g | mayo |
| 120 g | greek yogurt |
| to taste | salt and pepper |


- Put everything in a jar and blend with an immersion blender.


#### Source
It seems pretty much every cooking blog has a recipe for this, and after trying a few I've settled on these approximate amounts.

#### Notes
This is almost worth a preamble. I mostly do not like mayo, so where I use it, it is very intentional. Caesar salad made the traditional way is great: start with a raw egg and whisk in oil to create an emulsion (you've basically just made mayo), and flavoring it with anchovy, Worcestershire sauce, garlic, lemon, and pepper. Instead, you could just start with store bought mayo, thin it out, and flavor it up. Something about that sounds kinda unappealing to me for some reason. Instead, you could make the base from half mayo and half greek yogurt.
