| ---------- | ------ |
| egg yolks | 3 |
| sugar | 50 g (1/4 cup) |
| vanilla | 1 bean |
| cream | 1 cup (~225 g) |

1. Whisk egg yolks, sugar, and seeds from vanilla pod until light and smooth.
2. Whisk in cream.
3. Pour into four small ramekins and bake in hot water bath at ~275ºF for 30-60min.
4. Once cool, sprinkle with sugar and caramelize with a torch.

#### Source
Chef Richard at STARESO in Corsica.

#### Notes
- Original specifies 200g sugar, 10 yolks, 1 L cream, baked at 90ºC for 45-60min.
