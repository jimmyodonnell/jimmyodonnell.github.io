| Ingredient | Amount |
| ---------: | :----- |
| flour | 244g |
| baking soda | 1.5 tsp |
| ginger | 1.25 tsp |
| allspice | 0.25 tsp |
| cloves | 0.125 tsp |
| salt | 0.25 tsp |
| butter | 6 TBSP |
| brown sugar | 150g |
| molasses | 80g |
| eggs | 1 |
| vanilla extract | 1 tsp |
| coarse sugar | for rolling |


1. Melt butter in a large vessel and let cool
2. Combine dry ingredients
3. Mix butter, sugar, molasses, egg, and vanilla.
4. Stir in the dry ingredients
5. Roll into balls and roll balls in coarse sugar
6. Bake at 350 for 10-15 minutes



#### Source
- [Claire Saffitz](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EcfZ3PaqdeI) for the bones, but there are many similar recipes with only minor differences.


#### Notes
- I have subbed some of the dry powdered ginger with fresh grated and it was quite nice.
