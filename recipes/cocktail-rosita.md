### Ingredients

| ---------- | ------ |
| Tequila or Mezcal | 1.5 oz   |
| Sweet Vermouth | 0.5 oz |
| Dry Vermouth | 0.5 oz |
| Campari | 0.5 oz |
| Angostura Bitters | 1 dash |


### Method

1. Stir all ingredients over ice.
2. Strain into an old fashioned glass. Garnish with a lemon peel.

- **Mix**: Stir
- **Glass**: Old Fashioned
- **Over Ice**: Yes
- **Garnish**: Lemon peel
